About

Sales instincts, engineer's toolkit

21 years in front of clients. Five years learning to build what they need.

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in AI sales, consultation, and client services roles, applying my experience in AI data annotation, data auditing, and automation workflows, backed by 21 years in sales, account management, and customer service.

I came to Canada from India at 16 and have worked full-time since. After completing Grade 12 in Ontario, I worked in outbound telemarketing from 2000 to 2004, handling long-distance calling for Sitel, American Express, Gemma, and other call-centre campaigns. I then joined Bell from 2004 to 2006, where I was promoted to senior customer service agent within a year, received a $10,000 raise, and won several customer service awards. After six years of full-time work, I left Bell to update my skills and earned a Business Administration diploma at CDI College from 2007 to 2008, where I developed a full business plan for prospective investors and financing—an experience that strengthened my ability to translate client needs into practical recommendations.

At AlarmForce from 2008 to 2011, I was consistently one of the top agents, a record verifiable through manager references and CRA documents. It was during this time that I founded iBiz Marketing, an automated political-campaign outreach service. In 2011, we supported NDP campaigns with phone-based voter connection, candidate-voice messaging, pamphlet distribution, and needs-capture workflows. The NDP won overall that year, and the candidate’s vote count rose from roughly 6,000 to 13,000–14,000, suggesting the outreach had a good impact. I later worked at Choice Hotels before moving into B2B account management at Cansel Inc., where I achieved 100–120% of a semi-quarter sales objective of roughly $500,000. At AEC PropertyTax, I met 100–120% of booking and sales targets through outbound cold calling. Across these roles, I kept accurate Salesforce CRM records and worked with minimal direction.

I recently completed the final two-month stage of a four-year AI apprenticeship combining software engineering and model evaluation. Challenges rotated: email automation one day, and permutation and combination work for financial software using pandas, NumPy, or Stable Diffusion the next. I analyze model behaviour, apply NLP frameworks such as 5 Ws analysis and output comparison, and perform evaluate-and-refine work, dataset auditing, and verification.

I work with Python, C++, Java, REST APIs, and React Hooks, with hands-on AWS, Google Cloud, and Oracle Cloud experience. I also built automated media pipelines for captioning, downloading, and cross-posting across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and TikTok. Projects were handed off to senior engineers to assess how well I grasped requirements. I understand that lead generation is critical and that media and online presence must be fully active.

As AI tools shifted toward GitHub repositories and subscription cloud platforms, I learned both sides—Python, PowerShell, and C—and can build chatbots or voice bots for a fraction of the cost, maneuverable at any time. I use iBiz in my business and in a friend’s business to automate social posts and newsletters. I can demonstrate working programs on my Termux phone or at github.com/petemits, and my YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, and GitHub channels share project stories.

I am fluent in English and also speak Hindi and Punjabi. I understand the architecture of machinery, networks, and neural networks, and I am actively engaged in digital platforms. At 48, I have 18+ years ahead to grow in technology, IT, and cloud roles. I am comfortable in remote, hybrid, and on-site settings and am open to both contract and employee positions. Employment verification is available through T4 and CRA records.

I am entrepreneurial by nature and still hold that ambition, but I am currently seeking a company where I can grow within a team. The IT industry moves fast, and keeping up requires continuous exploration, self-directed learning, and collaboration. To complete this digital world—robots and all—takes a great many minds, and I want to be part of that.

I look forward to meeting with you and exploring further how I can build my career with your organization. I promise you a good return to fulfill the mission of your organization.

Sincerely,
Amit Puri
